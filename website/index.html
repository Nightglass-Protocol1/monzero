MONZERO / XMZ · OPEN-SOURCE DIGITAL CURRENCY

Privacy is a right.
Not a privilege.

Monzero (XMZ) is an independent, open-source cryptocurrency designed for private digital transactions, accessible CPU mining and community participation.

Its own blockchain uses RandomX proof-of-work, with mining designed for general-purpose CPUs.

Why Monzero

Built for privacy. Powered by people.

Monzero combines privacy-focused digital payments with an accessible proof-of-work network. Its design prioritises independent verification, open participation and user control.

Privacy at the Core

Ring signatures obscure which output is spent, one-time recipient addresses protect payment destinations, and confidential transactions hide amounts. Privacy depends on how the software and network are used.

Understand the protocol ↗

Accessible CPU Mining

RandomX is designed for general-purpose CPUs. Rewards and profitability depend on hardware, electricity costs, network difficulty and mining conditions.

Learn about CPU mining ↗

An Independent Blockchain

Monzero operates its own blockchain and native currency, XMZ. Participating nodes verify the chain under proof-of-work consensus.

    Community & funding

    Support the Future of Monzero

    Continued development depends on infrastructure, software maintenance and community participation. Support helps the project pursue broader network access and ecosystem improvements.

    Where support goes

    Current goals cover a proposed Biconomy listing and bug bounties. The listing is unconfirmed. The project's public funding discussion describes early fee figures as preliminary estimates; no formal fee quote is published alongside the current target, which also includes trading funds.

    The published policy retains funds for the next listing if this one does not proceed. Donations do not guarantee exchange acceptance or financial returns.
