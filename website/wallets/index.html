What these figures mean

Shared balances are snapshots reported by the wallet, not independently verified balances. A wallet signature proves control of the address; it does not prove the balance or the owner’s identity. Check the update time and wallet height before relying on a figure.

GenesisWallet1 is the permanently listed founder disclosure. Its initial figure comes from the published founder statement at block 9,838 and is not a live balance. This is the founder’s named wallet, not a special allocation embedded in the genesis block.

In a compatible wallet, choose Private, Share address, or Share address and balance. Publishing is explicit. Stop sharing removes an ordinary profile and stops future disclosure; it cannot erase copies or screenshots already made. No private view keys, seeds or spending keys are published.
