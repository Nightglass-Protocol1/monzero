How to read this estimate

Expected reward = your hashes per second × elapsed seconds ÷ observed difficulty × observed block reward × (1 − pool fee). A month means 30 days. The observed block reward can include transaction fees; it is not a promise of future emission or fees.

Difficulty and rewards change. Solo mining may produce long periods without a block; pool payouts depend on its rules, accepted shares, confirmations and minimum payout. Pool fees reduce expected rewards. Electricity costs continue even when no reward is found. Hardware depreciation, taxes and other costs are excluded.

Source: the existing Monzero public-node readiness service; inspect blocks in the explorer. Readiness requires a matching mainnet tip within 15 minutes of the server clock, allowing up to five minutes of future timestamp skew. This is an operational freshness policy, not a consensus rule. A single node observation is not independent verification of the entire network. No mining starts when you use this tool.
