Longest uptime and weekly or monthly activity need report history, which the reporting service does not keep: it stores only each miner's latest report and deletes it after 7 days without one. These boards will appear only if history is collected with the same opt-in.

Join or leave

The reporter runs next to your own node and sends only a random installation ID, your hashrate, a block count and whether you are mining. Ask in the community for a reporting token. To leave, stop the reporter or delete its token file. You disappear from this page within 3 minutes, and your record is deleted after 7 days.
