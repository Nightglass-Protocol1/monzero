XMZ FAUCET

Faucet

Status: paused

The faucet is not active and accepts no claims. It has not been approved, funded or security tested. Nobody can claim XMZ here, and any site or message offering “Monzero faucet” payouts in the meantime is not this one.

The quickest way to get XMZ today is to mine it with your CPU, alone or through the pool.

How it will work

When it opens, the faucet will send a small amount of XMZ to a new user's address, at most once per address within a cooldown period. You will never be asked for a seed phrase, private key or payment.

  • Payouts come from a separate wallet holding only a small balance. The website itself never holds keys.
  • Limits per address, per visitor and per day, plus an anti-bot check, stop anyone from draining it.
  • Every claim and payout is logged, and the faucet can be paused at any time.

The full design is in docs/FAUCET_ARCHITECTURE.md in the source repository. How it is funded will be announced before it opens.
