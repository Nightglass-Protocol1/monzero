Support the project · draft of 17 October 2026

Help fund an independent, CPU-mined privacy coin.

Monzero has no premine, no ICO and no developer fee, so nothing pays for servers, development or exchange listings except the people who want it to exist. This page lists what money is needed for, where to send it, and how every coin received and spent will be reported. A donation is a gift to the project. It does not buy XMZ, a share, a vote or any future return.

What the money is for

Three goals, funded and reported separately.

Every goal has its own target. Money is spent only on the goal it was raised for. When a goal is full, any extra goes to the next goal in this list, and the move is reported.

  1. 1. Exchange listings

    Most exchanges charge a listing fee, and a new market also needs starting funds so people can actually trade. Those starting funds are the project's own XMZ and coins, which are reported. Applications are made only once the network meets the readiness checks published with the application, and every fee paid is shown in the spending report with the exchange's name.

Check before you send

Signed address statement

The full list of donation addresses is signed with the Bitcoin donation address's key and published in three places: this page, the project's Bitcointalk thread and the GitHub repository. The three copies must match exactly. If they do not, do not send anything, and report it to security@monzero.org.

The signed statement will be published here before the page goes live.

Nobody will message you for money

The project never asks for donations in direct messages, on Telegram or by email, and never sends an address that is not on this page. Anyone who does is not us. There are no presales, private rounds or "early access" coins.

Transparency

Every coin in and out, in public.

What anyone can check

Bitcoin, Litecoin and Ethereum balances and payments are public on their block explorers.

Monero and XMZ are private by design, so their view keys will be published here. A view key lets anyone see what a wallet received, but it cannot spend anything. Spends from those wallets will be listed with their transaction IDs and proofs.

View keys: to be published before launch.

Commitments

What we promise donors.

  1. No returns, no tokens for money. Donations never buy XMZ or anything else, and no price or profit will ever be promised.
  2. Separate wallets. Donation wallets never mix with the founder's personal coins or mining rewards (see founder holdings).
  3. Spent on the goal. Money raised for a goal is spent on that goal, and any move between goals is reported.
  1. Reported monthly. Every spend appears in the report with its transaction ID.
  2. No paid promotion of price. Funds are never spent on paid "pumps", fake trading volume or paid price promotion.
  3. If the project stops, the remaining funds go to a named open-source privacy project, announced in advance. They never go to the founder personally.

Other ways to help

Money is not the only thing that helps.

Mine on the pool or run a public node. Test the wallet and report bugs. Review the code, or translate the guides. All of these make the network stronger, and none of them cost anything.
