What the money is for

Two goals, funded and reported separately.

Donations fill the goals in order: when a goal is full, the rest goes to the next one. Money is spent only on the goal it was raised for. The bars below update with the live total, valued in US dollars at today's prices.

  1. 1. Listing XMZ on Biconomy

    Several exchanges have approached us about listing XMZ, and the first we are working towards is Biconomy. Monzero is already listed in the Chainquiry project directory. Like most exchanges, Biconomy charges a listing fee. A new market also needs some coins on both sides at the start, so that the first buyers and sellers can actually trade. This goal pays for both.

    The fee is paid straight to Biconomy, never through an agent or middleman, and its receipt goes in the spending report. The trading funds remain project funds, are reported like every other spend, and are never used to push the price or fake trading volume. If the listing does not go ahead, the money stays here for the next listing.

Nobody will message you for money

The project never asks for donations in direct messages, on Telegram or by email, and never sends an address that is not on this page. Anyone who does is not us. There are no presales, private rounds or "early access" coins.

Transparency

Every coin in and out, in public.

What anyone can check

All six donation wallets are on public blockchains: Bitcoin, Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash, Dogecoin, Ethereum and Solana. Anyone can see every payment in and out on a block explorer, without asking us. The live figures above are read from those same explorers.

XMZ is private by design. To make the XMZ donation wallet checkable, its view key will be published here. A view key lets anyone see what the wallet received, but it cannot spend anything.

Commitments

What we promise donors.

  1. No returns, no tokens for money. Donations never buy XMZ or anything else, and no price or profit will ever be promised.
  2. Separate wallets. Donation wallets never mix with the founder's personal coins or mining rewards (see founder holdings).
  3. Spent on the goal. Money raised for a goal is spent on that goal, and any move between goals is reported.
  1. Reported monthly. Every spend appears in the report with its transaction ID.
  2. No paid promotion of price. Funds are never spent on paid "pumps", fake trading volume or paid price promotion.
  3. If the project stops, the remaining funds go to a named open-source privacy project, announced in advance. They never go to the founder personally.

Other ways to help

Money is not the only thing that helps.

Mine on the pool or run a public node. Test the wallet and report bugs. Review the code, or translate the guides. All of these make the network stronger, and none of them cost anything.
